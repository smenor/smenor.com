Bio

I am a scientist and technologist with a background in physics and microbiology/virology and a track record of applying first-principles thinking to complex problems. My work focuses on de-risking and delivering advanced systems, from foundational research in biophysics and evolution to practical applications in robotics, distributed computing, and hardware design.
