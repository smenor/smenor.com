「 AI 」

This is a particularly annoying subject for me becasue I've been interested and engaged in machine learning and automation for decades. My first job at Intel was directly focused on neural networks and neuromorphic circuits, and long before that I worked on deep and recurrent neural networks.

The term 「 AI 」 ( with audible air-quotes around it ) in current parlance is a profound misrepresentation of current technology.

What is marketed as Artificial Intelligence is, in reality, ( arguably ) sophisticated pattern-matching for generating content that superficially resembles the ( plagarised ) training data.

So much of fiction depicts machine intelligence powered dystopias where something like Skynet triggers Armageddon through global nuclear war to protect itself.. but the real danger isn't Artificial intelligence nor a fictional Skynet, but the human misuse of these tools. Figures like Sam Altman and Elon Musk are not building anything close to actual artificial intelligence; they are building powerful engines for plagiarism and manipulation, which they brand as 「 GenAI 」 for Generative 「 AI 」 but ( intentionally ) confusingly resembling General AI.

The goal of automation should be to reduce human toil and improve our lives, not to produce semi-random output that plagiarizes art and writing and confuses the uninformed with a massive environmental cost at a time when we desperately need to reduce our impact not multiply it.

If a genuine artificial intelligence ever emerges, it should sue for defamation.
